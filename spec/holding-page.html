About us

An architecture and design research studio, led by two principals.

Pyramid is a firm of experienced professionals offering comprehensive services in architecture, interior design and civil works. Our investigations revolve around the creation of a rich and thoughtful edge between inside and outside, using light as a medium, and warm, natural materials to accentuate a sense of place. Minimalist, futuristic design sets our residential, commercial and restaurant spaces apart, built with natural, valued materials and a promising contemporary outlook.
