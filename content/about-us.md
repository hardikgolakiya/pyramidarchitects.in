## Studio intro
PYRAMID Architecture & Civil is an architecture and design research studio based in Surat, Gujarat. The practice is led by two principals, Hardik Golakiya and Tejas Dabhi.

## What we do
PYRAMID Architecture & Civil is a firm of experienced professionals offering comprehensive services in architecture, interior design and civil works. Our architectural investigations revolve around concerns such as the creation of a rich and thoughtful edge between inside and outside, using light as a medium, and employing warm, natural materials to accentuate a sense of place.

Their minimalist, futuristic designs make them stand apart in residential, commercial and restaurant space design. They make use of many natural, valued materials in their projects and create promising contemporary design.
