PYRAMID Architecture & Civil is an architecture and design research studio based in Surat, Gujarat. The practice is led by two principals Hardik Golakiya and Tejas Dabhi.

PYRAMID Architecture & Civil is a firm of experienced professionals offering comprehensive services in architecture, interior design & Civil works. Our architectural investigations revolve around such concerns as the creation of a rich and thoughtful edge between inside and outside, utilizing light as a medium, and employing warm, natural materials in order to accentuate a sense of place. 

Their milimalistic, futuristic design make them stand apart in the residential, commercial and restaurant space design. They make use of a lot of natural, valued material in their various projects and create promising contemporary design.
